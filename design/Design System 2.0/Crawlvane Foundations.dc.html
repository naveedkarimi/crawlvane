{{ ty.name }} {{ ty.sample }} {{ ty.d }} {{ ty.m }}
  • Headings use negative tracking: −0.02em at Display and H1, −0.01em at H2 and 0 below that. Overlines use +0.08em, uppercase.
  • Keep body measure between 60 and 75 characters. Use text-wrap: balance on headings and pretty on paragraphs.
  • Never set text below 12 px. Interactive labels are at least 14 px. Mobile body text stays at 16 px so iOS doesn't zoom form fields.
  • Scores and prices use Figtree 800 with font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums.

Motion

--t-fast 120msHover, press, focus, and copy confirmation.
--t 200msAccordion, tabs, tooltips, and new rows in the activity log.
--t-slow 320msToast enter, and the score ring filling once on reveal.
--easecubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1). Movement is limited to 8 px, with no bounce or parallax.
Reduced motion With prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, all transitions run in 1 ms. Score rings and bars render at their final value. The progress spinner becomes a static "Running" label. The activity log appends rows without sliding and stops auto-scrolling, and a "Jump to latest" button appears instead.

Icons

{{ ig.el }}{{ ig.name }}
  • Keep using Lucide, outline only, with round caps and joins. Use 2 px stroke at 16 and 20 px, and 1.75 px at 24 px and above.
  • Icons take currentColor. Don't fill them in two tones or put them in coloured circles.
  • Status icons are fixed: circle-check for pass, triangle-alert for warning, circle-x for fail, info for info. Don't reuse them for anything else.
  • An icon-only button needs an aria-label and a 44 px hit area, even when the icon is 16 px.
  • Don't use the sparkles, bot, brain or wand icons.
