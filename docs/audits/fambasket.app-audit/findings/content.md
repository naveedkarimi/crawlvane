# Content Quality and On-Page SEO

## Evidence
- Title: `FamBasket – Shared Grocery List & Household App for Families`
- Meta description: `FamBasket is the shared grocery list app families actually use. Claim items in real time, get a 10-second undo on every delete, track your pantry, and get a geofence alert the moment you walk into the store. Free for up to 2 members.`
- Robots meta: `index, follow`
- One H1: `Shop together. Stress less.`
- Eight H2 headings create a clear product, feature, comparison, pricing, FAQ, and conversion structure.
- Visible copy explains the product, audience, differentiators, pricing, privacy position, accessibility, and app availability. It includes a named founder/about section and social/contact links.

## Findings

### Medium: product claims need supporting proof
The page makes strong claims around geofencing, offline operation, privacy, accessibility, and live synchronization. These are useful differentiators, but independent proof, documentation, screenshots tied to each claim, or a linked privacy/security explanation would strengthen trust and E-E-A-T.

### Low: heading copy has a minor text-join defect
The rendered H2 `Cheaper than the stuffyou bought twice last month.` contains `stuffyou` without a space. Fix the source text and check generated text extraction after deployment.

### Low: single-page topical breadth is concentrated
The homepage is rich for a launch page but currently carries nearly all topical relevance. Consider supporting pages for shared grocery lists, grocery shopping with roommates, family pantry tracking, and app privacy/accessibility, each with unique intent-matched content and internal links.

## Positives
The title and description are specific, the H1 communicates the benefit, copy is concrete rather than generic, and the FAQ addresses real adoption objections.
